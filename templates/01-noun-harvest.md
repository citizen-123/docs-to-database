# Worksheet 1: Noun Harvest

**Goal:** List the "things" your business keeps track of. In database terms these are called *entities*, but for now just think of them as nouns — customers, invoices, jobs, trucks, parts, whatever your world contains.

**Time:** 30–60 minutes
**You'll need:** Your spreadsheets open, plus your sent-email folder

---

## Part A: Brain dump

Set a timer for 10 minutes. List every *type of thing* your business deals with. Don't filter, don't organize. One per line.

Prompts to shake things loose:

- What do customers buy or hire you for?
- What shows up on an invoice?
- What do you schedule?
- What breaks, runs out, or needs reordering?
- Who do you pay, and what for?
- What do you look up when a customer calls?
- What has a status that changes? ("pending," "shipped," "paid," "overdue")
- What do you have a stack, folder, or filing cabinet of?

> **Example brain dump (plumbing company):**
> customers, jobs, quotes, invoices, payments, technicians, vans, parts, suppliers, purchase orders, service calls, warranties, permits, timesheets
